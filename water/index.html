About the model: a teaching simulation, not a forecast. Each square is a quarter section (160 acres). Every city starts as a small river town and grows into the land its students zoned, at the pace they chose. Squares are built, occupied, and (for farms) fallowed separately, so a city can boom, then empty out. Water use per square comes from Arizona measurements; shortages fall first on outdoor watering, then on farms, then on homes and businesses. The economy counts what occupied squares produce plus construction. A shrinking aquifer, deeper wells, and shortage years drive businesses and families away.

Sources and parameters
City of Phoenix / ADWR, Phoenix Metropolitan Area Multi-City Water Use Study (2019): single-family homes about 350 gal/day, 57% outdoors. Water Research Foundation multifamily study: 182 vs. 331 gal/day per unit in Phoenix. Univ. of Arizona, Toward Water-Resilient Agriculture in Arizona (2023), Table C1: alfalfa 5.4, lettuce 3.4–4.3, pecans 2–7 acre-feet per acre. ADWR: dairy allotment 105 gal per cow per day; turf 4.6 acre-feet per acre; incidental recharge 25% of irrigation (Phoenix AMA model, 2024). USDA NASS Arizona (2022): alfalfa $320/ton at 8.2 tons/acre; lettuce $965M on 65,000 acres; pecans 1,680 lb/acre at $2.59; milk $25.20/cwt. NAHB, The Economic Impact of Home Building in a Typical Local Area (2015): 100 homes = 394 jobs and $28.7M the year built, then 69 jobs and $4.1M a year. BEA/BLS: Phoenix metro output about $120,000 per job (2022). Dalhuisen et al. (2003), Land Economics 79(2): residential price elasticity −0.41. Hanson, UC ANR: 1.02 kWh per acre-foot per foot of lift at 100% efficiency. Mayer et al. (2007), J. Environ. Quality 36: riparian buffers remove nitrogen, more consistently when wide. ADWR (2023): Phoenix AMA model found 4.86 million acre-feet of unmet groundwater demand over 100 years, halting new groundwater-based subdivisions. Williams, Cook & Smerdon (2022), Nature Climate Change 12: the southwestern megadrought. Town of Queen Creek–GSC Farm Colorado River transfer (ADWR, 2021). City of Phoenix Council, TSMC development agreement (Nov. 18, 2020).
